# Origen coordinator pilot — partner quick guide

**Your role:** Help students prepare for college conversations between meetings. You see a selection each student chooses to publish to you, not their full binder or conversation history.

## Before inviting students

The pilot starts with one approved organization, two or three named coordinators, and five to ten consenting adults using their own student accounts. Your organization owner invites staff using their work email. Each person accepts their own invitation, verifies their email, completes MFA, and receives coordinator capability. Never share staff accounts. Agree on a response schedule and a named backup contact before enrollment; a suggested starting commitment is two business days, subject to your team's approval.

## Connect, then share

1. Open the coordinator workspace and create your connection code. Give it directly to the student, as text, a link or a QR code. A code identifies you; it grants no binder access. Rotating it stops new requests with the old code and does not cancel existing sharing.
2. The student enters the code in their binder or tells Origen the code. Origen can find you and prepare a confirmation card. The student checks your name and organization and presses **Confirm request**. Saying “yes” to Origen alone cannot send the request.
3. Accept the student's request. Their binder is still private.
4. The student opens **People with access**, chooses eligible notes, reviews exactly what you will see, and presses **Publish these entries**. New notes and later edits are not automatically shared. Publishing again replaces the selection and starts a new 90-day period.

## Respond in the binder

Open **Shared binders**, choose a student and topic, and select **Write guidance in this topic**. Write a short, specific suggestion or question; add an official resource link when helpful. The student sees your guidance in red ink with your name, organization and date. You cannot change their own notes. Edits remain attributable. Use **Mark this publication reviewed** to acknowledge the version you reviewed.

Students can explicitly reply to your guidance or privately discuss it with Origen. Only a reply they submit is shared with you. Check the in-app **Updates** panel on your agreed schedule; this pilot does not send email notifications.

## What stays private

Family conversations, parent-originated material, recordings, transcripts, private global summaries, uncertain-source notes, and unpublished material are excluded. The initial pilot shares only eligible new manual student notes. Do not ask a student to copy family conversations into a shareable note, or to share passwords, full identification numbers or financial account details. A shared summary only describes the published selection.

## End access or report a problem

Students can remove an entry or **Stop sharing with this person** at any time. Access also expires or ends when staff leave. Existing student copies of guidance can remain after sharing ends. The student can hide/remove guidance or use **Report**; a coordinator can withdraw their guidance. Ask the organization owner to remove departing staff immediately. Replacement staff need a new student connection and publication; access is never transferred automatically.

For access or privacy problems, use the partner support contact agreed at onboarding. Provide the time, affected screen and safe error/reference ID; do not send screenshots of private notes, passwords or connection codes. Pause use and contact the designated incident owner if unexpected private information appears. This is not an emergency or continuously monitored service.

**Before launch, fill in:** organization owner, coordinator response schedule, absence contact, support contact and incident escalation contact. Keep that contact sheet with the organization, outside public documentation.
