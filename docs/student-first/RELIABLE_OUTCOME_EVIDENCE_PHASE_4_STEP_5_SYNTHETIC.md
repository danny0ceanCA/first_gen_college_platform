## Coverage and missingness

The denominator retains pending, unknown and explicitly declined outcome reports when the episode itself has separate evaluation permission. Declining evaluation excludes the episode entirely and is not an outcome failure. Presentation and silence were not collected and cannot be inferred. Corrections count only changes observed after explicit opt-in, not historical correction frequency. Missing event dates do not support time-to-outcome estimates.

## Interpretation limits

- Voluntary participation and return/reporting behavior select this group; it does not represent every eligible student.
- Pending, unknown, declined outcomes and adverse results stay in the denominator when the episode is permitted.
- Repeated reports or observations do not create additional episodes or successes.
- No coordinator ranking, causal effect, partner export, research or model training is authorized.
- Small cells and their complements are suppressed; no arbitrary date, term, process or demographic filters are available.

Counts are descriptive quality measures. The selected group does not establish program effectiveness. Reports are generated on demand with current permissions; no episode dataset or report cache is retained. Threshold suppression is not a differential-privacy guarantee.
