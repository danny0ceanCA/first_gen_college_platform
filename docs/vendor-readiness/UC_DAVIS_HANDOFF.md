## Scope brief for an initial conversation

Origen Edu is being developed to help students and families explore education pathways. The proposed UC Davis service is a dedicated institution page containing university-approved information and links, plus institution-level monthly page-view and link-click totals.

The university reporting design excludes visitor identities, prospective-student leads, GPA, family profiles, private conversations, voice recordings and education plans. Application analytics use aggregate counters rather than individual event histories. Reports withhold counts below 10 and show completed calendar months. Repeat activity and bots may be included; counts do not represent unique people or applications.

The wider Origen platform includes family/student planning and AI features that process personal information separately. Those features must remain visible in the assessment architecture even though their records are excluded from university reports. No UC student-information-system integration or university-supplied applicant dataset is proposed for the initial service.

Institution pages have a draft, affiliation-review and publication process implemented in local source. University reporting is institution-scoped. Local tests and a preliminary public-page accessibility review have been completed. Deployment verification, independent assurance, full accessibility evaluation and provider evidence remain outstanding.

This is a request to discuss fit and review expectations. It is not a commitment to launch, an approved UC Davis affiliation or an assurance of P1/P2 classification.
