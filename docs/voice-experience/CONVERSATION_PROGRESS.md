## Reading the numbers correctly

First sound may be a listening sound or acknowledgment. A useful answer is a different measure. Context acknowledgment confirms estimated injection, not speech, intelligibility or understanding. Provider transcript intervals, browser playback and human listening are separate evidence. An answer that began over the caller must not be counted as a fast post-caller reply.

Different datasets, languages, accounts, infrastructure and test-driver limits prevent a single credible “naturalness improvement percentage.” Preserve each result's denominator and measured stage. Classifier accuracy, request latency and conversational usefulness are different questions.

## Proposed experiment: human, purposeful pauses

Daniel's customer-service observation: a person helping a family may naturally ask for a moment to review something and explain a short silence. This is a proposal only. It does not authorize a prompt, pacing, permission-flow or withholding change in the current release.

Example: parent asks about two college budgets. Origen says, “Okay—give me a moment to compare what each estimate includes.” The existing research/check begins; Decisions may identify that one simple comparison would help. When relevant information is ready, Origen gives the useful difference and its limitation. A brief question can be appropriate if the answer actually needs it. Ordinary follow-ups should remain immediate.

Possible benefit: an honest acknowledgment can make a necessary wait understandable and may give a relevant background cue time to arrive before the substantive answer. It does not make research faster or guarantee better Decisions classification. Repeated permission questions, filler, fixed artificial pauses and “still checking” after completion would recreate earlier problems.

The [official GPT-Live prompting guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/live-prompting#personality-during-tool-calls) supports a chosen conversational style during tools, short truthful progress updates and avoiding repeated status. Compare the current style against a purposeful-acknowledgment variant using the same English/Spanish scenarios. Measure useful-answer timing, interruption/duplicate rates, qualifying-fact delivery, cue timing and human-rated warmth. Count every acknowledgment in the total waiting experience; do not present it as completed help.

## Maintaining the record

For each release add: commit/tag, deployment state, enabled flags, dataset/harness version, language and sample count, full request/receipt/playback timeline, actual useful-answer onset, interruptions/repeats, required qualifications, failures, costs and evidence location. Record human impressions separately from measured findings. Keep raw private transcripts and credentials out of Git; publish de-identified summaries and store durable operational diagnostics under account access controls.
