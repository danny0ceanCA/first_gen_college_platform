IMPORTANT CONTENT DECISIONS

- The notice does not say a name is the only personal information. Email/identity information, educational context, questions, summaries and free-text records can also be personal information.
- Origen's lack of a raw microphone-recording archive is distinguished from audio transmitted to OpenAI and OpenAI's own retention. Written summaries normally save automatically after a qualifying conversation; users are not asked to approve every summary first. Saving a profile is a separate action.
- Family linking shares designated academic profile fields. It does not automatically share private summaries, private needs or private notes. Ending a link does not erase an independent copy already retained elsewhere.
- Referring a family does not grant a community coordinator access to private conversations. Planned coordinator summary sharing is omitted, rather than presented as available.
- University analytics are described as aggregate reporting. Deliberately submitted institutional inquiries are separately explained; they are not automatic disclosure of private history.
- Providers are named: OpenAI, Auth0/Okta, Render and Google Fonts. Resend and the undeployed guardian email workflow are omitted.
- AI is described as designed and instructed for college guidance, without a guarantee that every output will stay in scope or be correct.
