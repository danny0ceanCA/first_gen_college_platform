Privacy Notice
For students, families and community organizations
Review draft • October 15, 2026
Origen Edu helps students and families explore college options and organize their next steps. This notice explains
what information we process, how voice and AI features work, what we save, who can access information, and how
to request help or deletion.
Who we are
Origen Edu is operated by Blueprint Holdings LLC. In this notice, “Origen,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to Blueprint
Holdings LLC. Our privacy contact is daniel@origenedu.ai.
This notice covers our website, applications, college-guidance features and related support. It applies to students,
family members, institutional representatives and visitors. Features and controls can differ between the web
application and mobile versions.
The main points
We use information to provide college guidance, maintain your account and planning history, carry out sharing you
request, and operate and protect the service. We do not sell personal information or provide private student
profiles or conversations to advertisers for targeting across unrelated services.
A name is not the only personal information involved. Your account identifiers, email address, educational details,
questions and saved summaries can also identify you or describe your circumstances. Optional details can make
guidance more relevant; you can limit the details you choose to provide.
Origen does not keep an archive of microphone recordings. Live audio is sent to OpenAI for processing. Written
conversation summaries normally save automatically after a conversation with captured speech and a confirmed
student or family context ends, and may include personal context. OpenAI’s processing and retention are separate
from Origen’s saved records.
Linking family accounts shares designated academic profile information. It does not automatically share private
conversations. A community organization’s referral does not give its staff access to your private history. University
page analytics contain aggregate counts, not private student profiles.
Privacy questions and requests
Email daniel@origenedu.ai. Tell us what you need and which account the request concerns. Do not send passwords,
financial identifiers or identity documents in an initial email. The final section explains requests by students, parents
and other authorized people.

1. Information we collect and use
Account and authentication information
We process information such as a provided name, email address, account role and identifier from the sign-in service.
This lets us authenticate users, associate saved information with the correct account, provide support and protect
access. Authentication providers also process information needed for sign-in under their own terms.
Student profiles and family planning
Depending on what you enter, profiles may include a student’s name, school or education stage, GPA, interests,
activities, goals, colleges of interest, intended entry term, practical needs and notes. We use those details to
personalize guidance and organize planning. Free-text fields may contain additional personal information even when
the field does not specifically request it.
Family linking creates records of invitations, acceptance, participants and linked student profiles. These records let
us manage the academic information shared through the link. Only enter information about another person when
you have appropriate authority and any permission required for that use.
Conversations and saved work
We process typed questions, spoken statements, AI responses, relevant profile details and previous summaries to
provide guidance. Saved work may include conversation summaries, topic notes, college plans, action items,
worksheets, source links and dates. Progress features, where available, also record changes to academic
information, plan revisions and self-reported milestones.
These are user-provided or generated planning records. They are not independently verified school records,
admissions decisions or assessments of a student’s ability. A summary or worksheet may contain personal
information even if it is described as a summary of college topics.
Requests you submit and public institutional content
An available institution inquiry or event-request form may collect a name, question, topic, optional contact
information, event details and the permissions you submit. We use that information to deliver and manage the
request. Institutional representatives provide work contact details, roles, affiliations and page content. Information
submitted and approved for a public institutional page becomes public.
Technical records, feedback and support
We process operational information such as feature-use indicators, language preferences, session identifiers,
request timing, error categories and quality signals to maintain the service and troubleshoot problems.
Infrastructure providers may receive IP addresses, browser or device details and other request metadata. Support
and privacy requests include the information you send and records needed to respond. A support request does not
authorize publication of your story or testimonial.
Please limit sensitive details
College guidance does not require passwords, Social Security numbers, bank-account numbers, payment-card
details, tax documents or confidential medical records. Avoid entering or speaking those details. Share only the
personal context you want processed and potentially included in a summary.

2. AI, voice and conversation summaries
College-focused guidance
Origen’s AI is designed and instructed to discuss college-related topics, including college exploration, preparation,
admissions, applications, financial aid, scholarships, education financing and planning. Instructions do not guarantee
that every answer stays within scope or is accurate. Verify important requirements, deadlines, costs and eligibility
with the relevant institution or official source.
What goes to OpenAI
Relevant questions, conversation text, selected profile information and past summaries may be sent to OpenAI to
generate responses or summaries. The service may compare a generated summary with conversation text to
identify quality problems. Searches for public college information can include the question and relevant search
criteria. Automated filtering cannot guarantee that every personal detail is removed from a search.
Microphone audio and temporary text
Voice is optional and requires microphone permission. During an active session, audio is transmitted to OpenAI for
live processing. Speech recognition produces text used to respond and prepare summaries. Stop the session or
revoke microphone permission to stop further capture; this does not recall information already transmitted.
Origen does not retain raw microphone recordings in its account database or offer a recording archive for replay. It
temporarily processes conversation text and pending summaries to support a discussion and retries. Those
temporary buffers are distinct from saved account history. Ending a call does not necessarily discard its text before a
summary is generated.
Summaries normally save automatically
After you speak in a conversation about a confirmed student or family context, a written summary normally
generates and saves automatically when the call ends. You are not asked to approve each summary first. A summary
can include topics, goals, academic context, questions, next steps and personal circumstances you mentioned. It is
not necessarily anonymous.
During onboarding, summary generation can occur before a new student profile is saved. Attaching the summary to
the account may wait until that profile exists, and pending work may retry automatically. Saving or reviewing a
profile is a separate action from generating a summary. A call with no captured user speech, no confirmed context
or a processing failure may not produce a saved summary.
Provider retention, review and training
OpenAI’s published API policy states that API data is not used for model training by default unless the customer opts
in. Its default abuse-monitoring logs may contain content and are retained for up to 30 days, with longer retention
possible for legal or harm-prevention reasons. Additional storage rules depend on the feature and configuration.
Origen’s lack of an audio archive is not a promise of zero provider retention.
OpenAI describes limited access to stored API business data by authorized employees for support, abuse
investigation and legal compliance, and specialist contractors under confidentiality and security obligations for
abuse review. Deleting a saved summary in Origen does not itself delete every provider record.

3. Sharing and access
No sale of personal information
We do not sell personal information or give advertisers private profiles or conversations for targeting across
unrelated services. Limited disclosures occur to provide the service, carry out your requests, protect the service or
meet legal obligations.
Linked family members
A family link is created through an invitation and acceptance. It shares designated academic profile fields: student
name, education stage, school, GPA, interests, activities, goals, selected institutions and entry term. Linked
participants may be able to update those shared academic fields.
Family linking does not automatically share private conversation summaries, private needs or private notes. It is not
proof of legal guardianship or unrestricted authority over someone else’s records. A parent or family member does
not automatically receive an adult student’s private information.
Ending a link stops future synchronization through that link. It does not erase an independent copy of previously
shared academic information retained in another account or a file another person downloaded.
Community organizations and coordinators
A community organization’s referral, sponsorship or relationship with a family does not give its coordinators access
to that family’s private profiles or conversation history. A staff title or participation in a program is not authorization
to monitor a student’s conversations.
University and institutional analytics
Institutional page reports use aggregate activity, such as page-view and link-click counts. They do not provide a list
of students, names, email addresses, private profiles or conversations. Basic reports use completed calendar-month
totals and suppress counts below a minimum reporting threshold. They are not reports of individual academic
ability, engagement quality or admission readiness.
Campaign reports may count interactions with institution pages or events. These counts do not by themselves
identify a particular visitor. Infrastructure and operational logs are separate from the aggregate reports supplied to
institutions.
Institutions you deliberately contact
If you submit an available question or event request, the designated institution or department receives the
submitted information and relevant replies. This is separate from page analytics and does not include your private
conversation history. Permission to answer a request and permission for future outreach are separate choices where
offered.
You can use available controls to withdraw a request or unsubscribe. A transfer to another institution or department
requires the permission described in that flow. Withdrawal cannot automatically erase independent copies already
received by the institution.
Other disclosures
We may disclose information as needed to meet a valid legal obligation, investigate misuse, address a security threat
or protect lawful rights. If ownership of the service changes, information may transfer with it subject to applicable
privacy protections and notice obligations.

What Origen staff can see
Application permissions limit staff reports by role. Activity reports contain operational trends. Directory access can
include account names, identifiers, status and activity or conversation metadata. Quality-review access can include
diagnostic findings and associated account identifiers. These reporting permissions do not expose private summary
text, transcripts, notes, plans or worksheets.
Authorized technical operators may be able to access underlying systems for support, maintenance, recovery,
security or a legal obligation. Restrictions in a dashboard do not mean the underlying stored content is inaccessible
to every human.
Institutional reviewers can review submitted institutional content and representative information. Authorized
institution staff can see inquiries deliberately submitted to their own workspace. Institutional roles do not grant
access to private family conversations or unrelated departments’ request inboxes.
Cookies, local storage and temporary buffers
Authentication and application operation use browser or device storage. Preferences, experience indicators and
some preview records can persist after a tab closes. Temporary signed-in conversation text is held in browser

Clearing browser storage does not delete cloud records, and deleting a cloud record does not remove an exported
file. External college sites have their own practices when you visit or send information to them. Providers may
process information outside the place where you live.

5. How long information remains
Default retention and your choices
The default for saved conversation summaries is to keep them until you delete them. Profiles, independently saved
notes, plans and worksheets also remain until you delete the relevant records or close the account. There is no
automatic account-deletion schedule based solely on inactivity.
Where the Privacy Center is available, you can choose automatic deletion of summaries after 30, 90 or 365 days. A
shorter selection also applies to older saved summaries. Cleanup runs periodically, so removal is not necessarily
immediate at the exact cutoff time.

Backups, infrastructure and security logs, support correspondence and records needed for legal obligations follow
separate retention rules. They are not removed simply by changing summary retention. We do not promise
immediate erasure from every backup or provider system. Contact daniel@origenedu.ai about the records involved
in your request.

6. Deletion, students and privacy requests
Deleting information or closing an account
You can review and edit supported profiles, remove supported saved records, manage family links, stop microphone
access, and use available export and account-closure controls. Some actions require a recent sign-in. Family links
may need to be resolved before closure, and institutional responsibilities can require additional review.
Deleting a summary can also delete notes generated from that summary. It does not automatically erase
independent notes, plans, worksheets, exports or another account holder’s independent copy of previously shared
information. Where a control is unavailable in your version, contact us for help.
Closing an Origen account removes supported records from active application storage and creates a separate
request to remove the Auth0 sign-in identity. That identity request needs separate completion and verification; a
queued request is not completed deletion. Closing Origen does not delete an underlying Google account or every
provider security log. The deletion receipts and other exceptions described in the retention section may remain.
Students, families and community partners
Origen is designed to support students in ninth grade and above and their families. A school grade does not establish
a person’s age or prove authority over their information. A family link, an organization’s referral and a school’s
authorization are different. None should be treated as unrestricted permission to access a student’s private
conversations.
Parents and legal guardians can request review or deletion of a child’s information by emailing daniel@origenedu.ai.
You do not need to link accounts to make a request. Identify the account, your relationship and the requested action
without sending unnecessary sensitive documents. We may need additional information to establish your authority.
A request concerning an adult student requires that student’s authorization or another valid basis.
How to make a privacy request
Email daniel@origenedu.ai to request access, correction, a copy of information, deletion or help with available
controls. We may ask for information reasonably needed to confirm identity and authority and to find the relevant
records. Do not send passwords or detailed financial identifiers. We will explain any additional steps or limits that
apply to the request.
Privacy rights and response requirements depend on the law that applies to you and the information involved. An
authorized representative may make a request with appropriate proof of authority. This notice does not waive your
applicable rights. If you disagree with a response, contact the same address and ask for a review.
Security and changes to this notice
We use authentication, access restrictions and separation of private account records from institutional reporting to
help protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your sign-in credentials, avoid sharing
accounts and report suspected unauthorized access to daniel@origenedu.ai.
When our practices change, we may update this notice and its date. We will provide additional notice or obtain
additional authorization when required. School-directed use and school-supplied records may require separate
agreements; an institution’s public page does not by itself create such an agreement.
